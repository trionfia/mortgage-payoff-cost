How it works
All three strategies are compared over the remaining loan term. “Keep loan + invest balance” invests the current balance today and follows the scheduled loan payments. “Keep loan + extra monthly payment” starts with the same balance, invests it, subtracts each extra principal payment from the invested balance while the loan remains active, and invests scheduled payments after early payoff. “Pay off” uses the balance to clear the loan, then invests the scheduled payments. Payments are interest-only during the remaining interest-only period, then amortize over the remaining term. Adjustable loans keep the current rate through the remaining initial fixed period, then adjust every 6 or 12 months by the entered recurring rate change, subject to optional per-reset and lifetime limits. Investment returns are compounded monthly; annual portfolio tax drag and capital gains are applied as selected.

Wise financial decisions are made by understanding the finances. Estimates are for educational purposes.
