How it works
All three strategies are compared over the remaining loan term. “Keep loan + invest balance” invests the current balance today and continues the scheduled loan payments. “Keep loan + extra monthly payment” starts with the same balance, invests it, subtracts the extra payment from the invested balance each month while the loan remains active, and then contributes the regular monthly payment to investments once the loan is repaid early. “Pay off” uses the balance to clear the loan, then invests the freed-up monthly payment. Returns are compounded monthly, with deposits made at month-end.

Wise financial decisions are made by understanding the finances. Estimates are for educational purposes.
